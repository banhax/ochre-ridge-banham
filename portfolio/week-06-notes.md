## Three impossible things from the Gus interview
1. **Laser-guided rock blasting**, this technology doesn't exist and is extremely unrealistic
2. **Predictive ore grade AI**, even with the 40 years of geological data, 95% efficency is asking a lot
3. **High-speed inspection robots**, huge safety risk and liability, 60km/h is insanely fast for a mine

## Three feasible things from the Gus interview
1. **Robotic transportation system**, whilst possible, it still poses no real gain over utilising the existing conveyer belt systems
2. **Robot dog army**, while this is 'feasible', it is rather superfluous
3. **Robots communicating in a different language**, this is possible and already happens with various AI model interactions
